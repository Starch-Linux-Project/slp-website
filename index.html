Starch Linux

A simple, well equipped, versatile and pre-configured Arch installation for nerds, developers, content creators and Linux newbies alike.

Starch Linux software and artwork may be copied, modified, and redistributed under their respective licenses. Modified distributions must follow the Starch Linux name policy.

Linux Update Utility

A cross-distribution update utility with a simple interface. Supports pacman, apt, dnf, Flatpak, and Snap.

Fully open source under the GNU GPLv3. Read the license and notice.
